What to expect in your Assessment

Your Financial Wellness Assessment is a straightforward conversation about where you are today, what matters to you, and how to build a plan around it. There is no cost and no pressure to buy anything.

We will cover three things

1. Understand your why

We will talk about your goals, responsibilities, family, income and the future you are working toward.

2. Assess where you stand

We will take an honest look at your current protection, savings, investments and retirement planning. Bring any questions or statements you would like to understand better.

3. Build your blueprint

We will identify practical next steps across three pillars: protecting what you are building, building wealth deliberately, and preserving what you have earned.

Before we meet

  • Think about your most important financial goal.
  • Bring your questions. There are no silly ones.
  • If useful, have your policy or investment statements nearby.

The more I understand your situation, the more useful our conversation can be. Personalised recommendations only follow after understanding your objectives, financial situation and needs.
