Advice (0.50%) is what's paid to your advisor for ongoing service. Platform (0.35%) covers Inn8's administration. Fund (1.05%) covers the underlying asset manager. Together they make up your Total Expense Ratio — what your investment actually costs you per year, deducted before returns are shown to you.

75% max equity · 45% max offshore

Regulation 28 of the Pension Funds Act caps how much of a retirement fund can sit in growth assets — a maximum 75% in equities and 45% offshore — to limit how much risk your retirement savings can carry.

Vested: R180,400 · Non-vested: R318,360

Vested amounts were saved before 1 September 2024 and follow the old pre-reform withdrawal rules. Non-vested amounts are new contributions, split across the Savings and Retirement Pots under the Two-Pot system.

02 · Five Numbers To Check

Before you file it away, look for these.

1

Total Expense Ratio

The single number that tells you what you're actually paying, all-in, every year.

2

Growth vs Contributions

Is Market Value meaningfully ahead of Book Value, or has growth barely kept up?

3

Offshore Exposure

Are you near the Regulation 28 offshore cap, or sitting well under it?

4

Vested vs Non-Vested Split

Knowing which rules apply to which portion matters before you touch any of it.

5

Beneficiary Nomination

Is it current? Life changes — marriage, kids, divorce — but nominations don't update themselves.
