Client Simple View

Three ways SARS treats your money.

No sections, no acts — just what happens to your tax bill, grouped by how each product behaves.

Column 1

Saves You Tax Now

Every rand you put in reduces what SARS taxes you on this year.

  • • Gateway Retirement Annuity
  • • Retirement Fund contributions

Column 2

Grows Tax-Free

No tax on growth, and no tax on the eventual payout either.

  • • Tax-Free Savings Account
  • • Life Cover & disability lump sums

Column 3

Pays Tax Later, But Less

Tax is deferred or paid on your behalf, usually at a lower rate than your own.

  • • Gateway Endowment
  • • Living & Life Annuity income

The right mix between these three columns is the whole point of a plan — not any single product on its own.
